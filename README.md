# Vela

### Web Designer & Developer

I create modern, responsive websites for small businesses, brands, creators, and personal projects.

## What I Do

* Landing Pages
* Business Websites
* Online Shops & Product Catalogs
* Interactive Website Features

## My Process

### 01 — Discovery

We discuss your business, goals, style, pages, features, and the type of website you need.

### 02 — Planning

I organize the structure, content, visual direction, and functionality before development begins.

### 03 — Development

I build the website, make it responsive across screen sizes, and add the agreed features and interactions.

### 04 — Review

You receive a working preview of the website so you can see how everything looks and works.

### 05 — Revisions

I make the agreed changes and polish everything based on your feedback.

### 06 — Launch

Once everything is approved, there are two options:

**Option A — You already have a domain and hosting**

You provide access to your existing hosting and domain setup, and I help connect and deploy the finished website.

**Option B — You don't have a domain or hosting yet**

You purchase your own domain and hosting from a provider of your choice. I can guide you through the setup and deploy the website once everything is ready.

### Important

The **website development fee** covers the work I do to design and build your website.

Your **domain name and hosting are separate costs**, paid directly by you to the provider. These are ongoing services that remain under your ownership and are renewed according to the provider's terms.

Once the website is deployed, you'll have a **live URL** that your customers can visit.

## What I Need From You

To build the website around your business, I'll need the relevant materials and information from you, such as:

* Your logo, if you have one
* Brand colors, fonts, or other brand guidelines
* Text and information about your business
* Photos, product images, or other visuals
* Product or service details and pricing
* Contact information and social media links
* Any specific features or functionality you want
* Examples of websites or designs you like

You don't need to have everything figured out before contacting me. We can discuss what you have and what the website needs during the discovery stage.
